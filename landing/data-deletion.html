Data Deletion Request

Last updated: June 2026

You have the right to request deletion of all personal data Socialline holds about you, including your account, connected social media credentials, scheduled content, and posting history.

What we delete

  • Your account credentials (username, email, password hash)
  • All connected social media access tokens and account identifiers
  • All scheduled posts, captions, and content you created
  • Your posting history and analytics data
  • Any uploaded media files

How to request deletion

1

Send us an email

Email your deletion request to the address below. Include the email address or username associated with your Socialline account.

2

We verify your identity

We will confirm your identity to ensure only you can request deletion of your data. We may ask you to verify ownership of the email address.

3

Deletion completed within 30 days

We will permanently delete all your data within 30 days and send you a confirmation. Some anonymized aggregate data may be retained for security logs.

Send your request to hello@socialline.space

Revoking platform access directly

You can also immediately revoke Socialline's access to any connected social media platform without contacting us — go directly to each platform's app settings:

  • Facebook / Instagram: Settings → Security → Apps and Websites → Remove Socialline
  • X (Twitter): Settings → Security → Apps and Sessions → Revoke access
  • TikTok: Settings → Privacy → Manage app permissions
  • LinkedIn: Settings → Data Privacy → Third-party applications
  • Google (YouTube): myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps → Remove access
  • Pinterest: Settings → Security → Apps → Remove access

Revoking access on the platform prevents Socialline from publishing to that account going forward, but does not delete data stored within Socialline. To delete stored data, please use the email process above.

Questions

If you have any questions about data deletion or our privacy practices, please review our Privacy Policy or contact us at hello@socialline.space.
