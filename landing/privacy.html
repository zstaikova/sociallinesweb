Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Socialline ("we," "us," or "our") operates the social media scheduling and publishing platform available at socialline.space and app.socialline.space. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Username, email address, and password when you register.
  • Connected platform credentials: Access tokens and account identifiers for social media platforms you connect (Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, YouTube, Bluesky, Threads, Telegram). These are stored encrypted and used solely to publish content on your behalf.
  • Content you create: Posts, captions, images, videos, and schedules you create within the platform.
  • Usage data: Log data including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps for security and debugging purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the scheduling and publishing service you have requested.
  • To authenticate and publish content to connected social media platforms on your behalf.
  • To send service-related communications (e.g., failed post notifications).
  • To improve and maintain the platform.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Platforms

When you connect a social media account, we interact with that platform's API under your authorization. Your use of connected platforms is also subject to their respective privacy policies:

  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads): facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • X (Twitter): twitter.com/privacy
  • TikTok: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • LinkedIn: linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google (YouTube): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Pinterest: policy.pinterest.com/privacy-policy

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secured servers. Social media access tokens are encrypted at rest using industry-standard encryption. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or connected account credentials with third parties except as required to operate the service or comply with law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Connected platform tokens are deleted when you disconnect a platform or delete your account. Content and schedule history may be retained for up to 90 days after account deletion for backup purposes.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (see our Data Deletion page).
  • Disconnect any connected social media platform at any time through the platform settings.
  • Revoke Socialline's access to your social media accounts directly from each platform's settings.

7. Cookies

We use session cookies solely to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

Socialline is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, contact us at: hello@socialline.space
