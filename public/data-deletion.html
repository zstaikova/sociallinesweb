Data Deletion Request

How to request removal of your data associated with Socialline.

Facebook & Instagram login data

When you connect a Facebook or Instagram account, we store only the access tokens and account identifiers needed to publish content on your behalf. We do not store your personal profile, posts, messages, or any other Facebook / Instagram data.

How to delete your data

  1. Remove app permissions via Facebook
    Go to Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites, find Socialline and click Remove. This immediately revokes all tokens we hold.
  2. Contact us directly
    Email hello@socialline.space with subject "Data Deletion Request". We will remove your account and all associated data within 30 days and send you a confirmation.

What we store

  • OAuth access tokens for platforms you connect
  • Platform account identifiers (e.g. Facebook Page ID, Instagram Business Account ID)
  • Content you upload (images/videos in your queue)
  • Post history (captions, scheduled times, publish results)

All data is stored on our private server and is never sold or shared with third parties.
