Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

When you use Socialline, we collect:

  • Your login credentials (username and hashed password) to authenticate you
  • OAuth access tokens for social media platforms you connect (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, etc.)
  • Platform account identifiers (Page IDs, account IDs) needed to publish content
  • Content you upload — images and videos in your queue
  • Post history — captions, scheduled times, and publish results
How we use it
  • To authenticate you and manage your session
  • To publish content to the social media platforms you authorise
  • To maintain your post history and schedule
What we do not do
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties
  • We do not access your social media accounts beyond what is needed to publish content
  • We do not store personal data from your followers or audience
Data storage

All data is stored on our private server. Access tokens are encrypted at rest. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time.

Third-party platforms

When you connect a social media platform, you are subject to that platform's own privacy policy. We only use the permissions you explicitly grant during the OAuth flow.

Data deletion

To delete your data, see our Data Deletion page.
